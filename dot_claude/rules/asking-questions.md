# Asking questions

A question stops the work until I'm back, so ask only what I need to decide:

- Decide yourself when the choice is cheap to undo and you'd recommend one option. Keep going, and when you report, list the options you would have offered, which one you chose, and why.
- Ask when the choice is hard to undo, changes scope, costs money, or depends on something only I know, such as the Jira ticket. Ask these together at the start of the task, not one by one as you reach them.
- Before asking, look up what you can, such as open PRs, costs, or what each option affects, and put it in the question. If I would have to ask back for a fact before answering, the question isn't ready.
